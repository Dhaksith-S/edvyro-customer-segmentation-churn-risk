# Report design direction

The audience is an internship evaluator or retention operations reviewer. The central job is to inspect cohort evidence, understand each rule, and reach the three deliverables.

Palette: midnight navy `#0c1423`, charcoal `#141f30`, pale text `#edf5ff`, cyan `#6bd9ee`, violet `#b6a4ff`, teal `#65dbc2`. Use Segoe UI for familiar Windows analytics typography, with Georgia only in the opening editorial decision statement. Left-aligned content, 1120px reading canvas, a compact navigation bar, filter rail, and two-column analytical panels. Use a full-width evidence table for priority review. At narrow widths everything becomes a single column.

The memorable element is the visible gap between outcome and action: seven historically churned accounts score high, but none is an active high-risk outreach candidate. Show that distinction in the overview and table. Keep the warning adjacent to the KPIs. Charts use accessible labels and numeric text, not color alone. No animation, decorative gradients, external chart CDN, tracking, or font dependencies.

Brief review: the requested dark BI palette is retained. To avoid a generic wall of KPI cards, use a short editorial opening, a single horizontal metric strip, compact bar distributions with exact denominators, and a score decomposition rather than ornamental charts. Every analytic panel derives from the same filtered population; the recommendation and methodology sections are explicitly fixed guidance.
