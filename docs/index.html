Investigate, then test.

Fixed recommendations from the full sample. These hypotheses do not change with filters and are not established intervention effects.

First investigation

Onboarding and service

Six first-year flexible accounts churned, with a mean of 4.50 support tickets. Review themes and unresolved issues. Test assisted onboarding in a new, active cohort.

Measure: 90-day churn and repeat tickets against randomized usual service.

Active cohort monitoring

Value and renewal review

Four one-year accounts are retained and score Medium. Collect renewal dates before scheduling any outreach; zero observed churn is weak evidence in a group of four.

Measure: renewal rate, net retained margin, complaints and opt-outs.

Maintain and learn

Service continuity

Four two-year accounts are retained and score Low. Maintain service and sample feedback. Review the single older flexible churn case qualitatively.

Measure: service satisfaction and contact cost; avoid untested discounting.

No failed-payment evidence exists. Do not infer billing friction or penalize UPI, cards, or bank transfer from these comparisons.

Every point has a rule.

Index v1.0 adds three observable account signals. Weights reflect documented planning assumptions. They were not statistically fitted or tuned to churn labels.

Tenure 0–35 + Commitment 0–45 + Charges 0–20 = 0–100

Low 0–29 Medium 30–59 High 60–100

Tenure contribution

0–5 months: 35 points. 6–12: 25. 13–24: 10. 25+: 0. Fixed onboarding and annual-review heuristics.

Contract contribution

Month-to-Month: 45 points. One Year: 20. Two Year: 0. Shorter commitment permits easier exit; this is not a causal estimate.

Charge contribution

Below INR 70: 0 points. INR 70–<100: 10. INR 100+: 20. These cutoffs split observed price tiers, not validated affordability bands.

Missing inputs and excluded features

Missing/invalid tenure, contract or charges gives no score and an Unscored band. Churn, support counts, payment method, age, gender, identifiers and total charges never enter the score. Outcome labels are used only after scoring.

What the evidence cannot tell us

Fifteen synthetic records cannot establish production performance. Risk bands exactly reproduce contract cohorts, adding no demonstrated outcome separation beyond contract alone. The 7/8 label split, tiny subgroups, correlated account features, uncertain snapshot timing, and absent observation dates prevent independent or temporal validation. There are no treatment outcomes, margins, payment failures or usage histories. Before operational use, verify all inputs precede churn and run a controlled prospective test.

Primary definitions: flexible first year = monthly contract and tenure ≤12 months; flexible beyond first year = monthly and >12 months; annual = One Year; two-year = Two Year. Missing tenure/contract is assigned Data review. Groups below five accounts are small samples.
